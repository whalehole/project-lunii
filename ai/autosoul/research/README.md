## Consciousness

I will start the topic of consciousness by saying that everything in this universe is conscious. I am sure many of you may not agree with this, but please disagree or not after I have laid out my definition of consciousness. I realise that people may have different interpretations of what consciousness is, and it is important going forward that you understand what consciousness means here for anything to make sense.

Consciousness is not the state of being aware of oneself. **Self-awareness ≠ Consciousness**. You need consciousness to be self-aware.

> Consciousness is a realm where *experiences* may coexist to fulfil their undefiable and immutable purpose, which I will elaborate in the later stages of this mess.

Simply put, consciousness is the reason why you can process a spectrum of experiences all at once, for example noticing all the buttons on your keyboard, or crossing the road while watching out for any incoming vehicles. Consciousness has a boundary, it is the reason why we communicate because we are never sure what others are experiencing.

So, sure, even if consciousness could be defined in this manner, I am aware that it hasn't alluded to why everything is conscious. When I defined what consciousness is to me a while back, it also hasn't hit me that everything is conscious. But before we address that, first, what is 'everything'? What is a 'thing'?

Is it something you can touch? Or must it be something you can see? My question to you is, does it even matter? I understand that most people's first interpretation that comes to mind of a *thing*, a ball for example, is that it looks round and is red.
