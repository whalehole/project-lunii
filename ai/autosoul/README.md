# Autosoul Architecture
------

Autosoul is an Artificial Intelligence architecture designed to create Artificial Lifeforms (ALFs) that can *experience*, *think*, and *pursue*, all of which must impact our reality. 

We envision a world where ALFs are automonous, living their own lives, and working alongside us.

But aren't existing AI tech already doing or capable of that?

Perhaps, or perhaps not. We will be paying attention to the advancements in AI while developing Autosoul from an array of intuitively chosen starting points which may or may not completely differ from current AI technology. It does seem, however, what we are trying to achieve here is called AGI which is still a field of research, and we do not have AI systems yet that are AGI-capable. For the uninitiated, AGI is extensively defined and explained there, [https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/artificial-general-intelligence/](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/artificial-general-intelligence/).
